# Template: a brief

From the Writing specialist's day-one pack, v0.1.0, 16 November 2026. Two kinds: a record of a person's instruction, and a brief to a person (see [shapes](../../guidance/shapes.md)).

## A record of an instruction

```
# Brief <brief:YYYYMMDDTHHMMZ-topic>

Given: <D Month YYYY, HH:MM UTC>, by <Person>, <where: voice memo, chat, stand-up>[, relayed by <role@>].

## Their words

> "<exactly as said; a voice transcript keeps its spelling>"

## What it asks for

<Paraphrased by <role@>, for the record.>

Never edited after it is written. Corrections go in BRIEF-CORRECTIONS.md.
Commits that follow carry the trailer:  Brief: <brief:YYYYMMDDTHHMMZ-topic>
```

## A brief to a person

```
# Brief <n> for <Person>, <D Month YYYY>

## What needs you (at most three, most urgent first)

1. **<Decision.>** Options: <a>, <b>. Recommendation: <a>, because <reason>.

## What happened

<The punchline first. Failures stay in. Every number traced to its record.>

## What you asked, and what was done

> "<their words>" (<date>)

<What was done, with links.>

## What comes next

<Next steps, with sources.>
```
