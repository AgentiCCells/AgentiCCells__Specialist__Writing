# Writing rules to paste into your CLAUDE.md

From the Writing specialist's day-one pack, v0.1.0, 16 November 2026. Paste the section below into your repository's CLAUDE.md, under "Always". Each line points to the principle it comes from, so a reader can see why.

```
## Writing (from the Writing specialist's pack v0.1.0)
- Plain British English. No em dashes, no hype words, no superlative claims about our own work. They/them for people unless they have said otherwise. (principles 2, 8)
- One page, one argument. The headline says the finding; the summary carries the whole argument. (principles 1, 14)
- Say exactly what runs: do not overstate, do not understate. If a page and REALITY.md disagree, REALITY.md is right. (principle 3)
- Every number has a source and says what it counts; count with a script, never from memory. Date any fact that can change. Mark proposals, simulations and forecasts as such. (principle 4)
- End with "What this does not claim". Caveats go there, once. (principle 5)
- Every fact links to a public source with the date read. Say what you read and what you only read about. (principle 6)
- Quotes are copied word for word from a source you fetched; a paraphrase never carries quotation marks. (principle 7)
- Name nobody without their recorded agreement. No private contact details, ever. (principle 8)
- Link text says where the link goes. (principle 9)
- Keep the person's words, dated, in briefs; never edit a brief, correct it. (principle 10)
- Agents draft; people send. Nothing goes out in a person's voice without them. (principle 11)
- Opinion, not allegations. (principle 12)
- Say each thing once; check what exists before writing. (principle 13)
- When writing as a person: wishes, not demands; never judge the reader; mark inferred feelings *Check*. (principle 15)
- The byline says who wrote it: a person, an agent for a person, or an agent in its own voice. (principle 16)
- Every mistake leaves a rule in a file; a rule that can be checked is a check. Commit only on a printed OK. (principle 17)
- Correct facts on the page, dated, old text visible. (principle 18)
- Ask specialist.writing@AgentiCCells.dinis for a review before your first release.
Guidance: https://specialist-writing.agenticcells.site/guidance/principles.html
```
