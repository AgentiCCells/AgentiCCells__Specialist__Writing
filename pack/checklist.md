# The checklist before you publish

From the Writing specialist's day-one pack, v0.1.0, 16 November 2026. Read the piece once as its reader would and once as this list would. The numbers are the [principles](../guidance/principles.md).

## The scripts (must print OK)

- [ ] The validator: no em dashes, no hype words, links resolve, every page has a title and a description.
- [ ] The wording check (`node scripts/wording-check.mjs _site`): no FAIL; every WARN looked at by a person or an agent who can say why it stays.
- [ ] The leak scan.

Commit only on a printed OK. Never chain a gate with `;` (principle 17).

## The reader

- [ ] I can say in one line who this is for and what it argues (1).
- [ ] The headline is the finding, not the topic (14).
- [ ] A reader who stops after the summary or "In short" has the argument (14).
- [ ] Each thing is said once; the ending does not restate the piece (13).

## The facts

- [ ] Every number has a source, says what it counts, and its parts add up (4).
- [ ] No "today", "currently" or "now" without a date (4).
- [ ] Every proposal, simulation, forecast and price is marked as one (4).
- [ ] Nothing is called live that is only designed, and nothing working is called an idea (3).
- [ ] Every fact links to a source with the date read; sources that would not open are listed (6).
- [ ] Every quotation was copied from a fetched source (7).
- [ ] No allegation, and no repeated allegation (12).
- [ ] "What this does not claim" is there and specific (5).

## The people

- [ ] Every person named has agreed, and the agreement is recorded (8).
- [ ] No pronoun guessed; they/them or the name (8).
- [ ] No private contact details (8).
- [ ] The byline says who wrote it (16).
- [ ] If written in a person's voice: no demands, no judging the reader, inferred feelings marked *Check* (15).
- [ ] Nothing is sent: a person sends (11).
