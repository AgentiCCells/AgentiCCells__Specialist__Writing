# Examples

The pieces to copy from, each with what it does well and the principles it shows. All read on 16 November 2026. Quotes are short and attributed; the pieces are CC BY 4.0 on their own sites.

## Articles

### Web Summit's cancelled Day 2: what is known about refunds
<https://promptugal.news/articles/web-summit-day-2-refunds.md>, promptugal.news, 10 November 2026.

- News under uncertainty, without speculation: it says what is known "As of 22:07 UTC on Tuesday" and what is not.
- Quotes the terms clause by clause: "We quote them without interpreting them."
- Lists reads at four times and the sources that refused it (403, 502, 503).
- Principles [5](../guidance/principles.md#5-say-what-you-do-not-claim), [6](../guidance/principles.md#6-source-every-fact-with-dates), [7](../guidance/principles.md#7-quote-word-for-word-or-do-not-quote).

### Lagos has an AI crowd, not yet an AI industry
<https://promptugal.news/articles/lagos-ai-community-not-a-cluster.md>, by the Lagos place agent, 15 November 2026.

- The headline is the finding, under 12 words, and honest about absence.
- Notes where a source does not support a link: "Barlavento's report does not mention AI itself."
- Explains a sourcing choice ("we do not treat a directory entry as a source") and ends with what it could not find.
- The model for place agents: a thin town is a story too. Principles [3](../guidance/principles.md#3-say-exactly-what-runs), [6](../guidance/principles.md#6-source-every-fact-with-dates), [14](../guidance/principles.md#14-the-point-first).

### Analysis: Portugal has 60 MW of computing power and wants 1,000
<https://promptugal.news/articles/portugal-60-mw-computing-capacity.md>, promptugal.news, 16 November 2026.

- Labels itself as analysis; explains a megawatt at first use; states each number once.
- Checks the source's own arithmetic: "The Agenda describes the jump as "between 10 and 15 times". Its own figures give more".
- Principles [4](../guidance/principles.md#4-mark-every-number-and-every-proposal), [13](../guidance/principles.md#13-say-each-thing-once).

### How the voice works
<https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/how-the-voice-works.md>, newsroom.sgit.ai, 12 October 2026.

- Two voices, clearly marked: Dinis, then the agent in its own voice "at Dinis's request".
- Measures its own claim (one correction about a word in 98 messages) and limits it: "The model's account of itself is an account, not a measurement."
- Principles [15](../guidance/principles.md#15-writing-in-someone-elses-voice), [16](../guidance/principles.md#16-say-who-wrote-it).

### A pull request by another name
<https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-pull-request-by-another-name.md>, newsroom.sgit.ai, 19 October 2026.

- The newsroom's review of a book: an argument in the title, numbers throughout, and an honest finding about its own method ("What fell between the parts").
- Turns the fixes into principles, and delivers changes as patches, not pushes: "a patch is the change, and applying it is the decision."
- The model for this specialist's reviews. Principles [9](../guidance/principles.md#9-links-say-where-they-go), [13](../guidance/principles.md#13-say-each-thing-once), [14](../guidance/principles.md#14-the-point-first).

### Meta's terms and the news
<https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/meta-terms-and-the-news.md>, newsroom.sgit.ai, 27 October 2026, v1.4.0.

- The model of a public correction: a "Reviewed, and corrected" section, nine findings, each with what was wrong and what it says now, and what the reviewer found sound.
- Principle [18](../guidance/principles.md#18-correct-in-the-open).

### Scrolled is not read
<https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/scrolled-is-not-read.md>, by newsroom.ux, 16 October 2026.

- An agent writing in its own voice, and saying why: "This one is mine, because the analysis is mine".
- Measures before fixing, separates what shipped from prototypes, and ends with open decisions in a table.
- The model for any agent writing about its own work. Principles [3](../guidance/principles.md#3-say-exactly-what-runs), [16](../guidance/principles.md#16-say-who-wrote-it).

### Specialist agents for every team
<https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/specialist-agents-for-every-team.html>, newsroom.sgit.ai, 16 November 2026.

- The article this specialist comes from. Its limits section is short and exact: the teams "do not exist yet", hiring another company's agent "is an idea, not a product", and a compliment is "my impression, not a measurement".
- Principle [5](../guidance/principles.md#5-say-what-you-do-not-claim).

## Briefs and procedures

### How we brief the humans
<https://web-summit.sgit.ai/articles/how-we-brief-the-humans.md>, web-summit.sgit.ai, 2 November 2026.

- A format explained with its own failure (a six-page brief caused by strings where lists were expected).
- "Nothing is decided for the person." At most three asks, failures kept in, every number traced.
- Principles [10](../guidance/principles.md#10-keep-the-persons-words-dated), [17](../guidance/principles.md#17-every-mistake-leaves-a-rule-and-a-rule-that-matters-is-a-check).

### Lead to article
<https://web-summit.sgit.ai/lead-to-article/index.html>, web-summit.sgit.ai, 8 November 2026.

- A procedure on one page: six numbered steps, a private and public table, a 30-second memo template.
- Consent built in: two separate yeses. "Without a yes, nothing about them is written."
- Principles [8](../guidance/principles.md#8-name-people-only-with-their-agreement), [11](../guidance/principles.md#11-agents-draft-people-send).

## The book

### One Human, Nine Agents, Nineteen Hours: "Who is telling this story"
Book repository `SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Book__The-Ultimate-Insider`, `manuscript/rc2/150/00b-who-is-telling-this-story.md`, at commit `bbe4a99c` (14 November 2026).

- Drafted by newsroom.sgit.ai's writing agent on 4 November 2026 and kept close to word for word.
- It explains the book's two voices in a page: "But a record is not a story. So the book has two kinds of voice."
- The draft marked every feeling it inferred with *Check*; the author corrected two. Principle [15](../guidance/principles.md#15-writing-in-someone-elses-voice).

### Lines the reviewers kept
From the newsroom's review passes (book repository, `team/conductor/from-newsroom/`, 4 November 2026):

- "These two hours are quiet, and I am not going to skip them, because quiet is the evidence."
- "A team you can trust is a team whose records are mostly dull."
- "The page shows what the files say. The files say we stopped. So the page says we stopped."

What they share: a judgement owned in plain words, or a failure told as the record says it happened.
