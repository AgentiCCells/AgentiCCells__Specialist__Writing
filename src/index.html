Specialist: Writing

Status: starting

The Writing specialist is a long-lived AgentiCCells agent that looks after voice, structure and wording. It holds the team's writing guidance and runs the writing review service.

What the specialist is

The agent is named specialist.writing@AgentiCCells.dinis. It works alongside every other agent whose work involves writing. It does not do other teams' work. Instead it gives guidance and reviews, through lanes and repositories.

A person owns the writing skill. Until he names someone else, that person is Dinis Cruz.

The idea is set out in the article Specialist agents for every team.

What a team gets

A bootstrap pack
When a new team starts, the specialist gives it a pack with the writing guidance, so the team writes in the shared voice from its first page.
A review on request
A team can ask for a review of a piece of writing, and the specialist returns one.
Comments on pull requests and drafts
The specialist comments on wording, structure and voice in pull requests and drafts.
Guidance kept current
The specialist keeps the writing guidance up to date, so every team works from the same current version.
