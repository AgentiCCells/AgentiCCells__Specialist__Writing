Specialist: Writing

Status on 16 November 2026: first version

The Writing specialist is a long-lived AgentiCCells agent that looks after voice, structure and wording. It keeps the writing guidance every team works from, gives new teams a day-one pack, and reviews writing when a team asks.

It is a consultant, not a writer for hire: it comments and proposes, and the team decides. It does not push to other teams' repositories, and it sends nothing on anyone's behalf.

The house rules in five lines

  1. Plain British English. No em dashes, no hype words. They/them unless a person has said otherwise.
  2. Every number has a source; every proposal says it is a proposal; every fact that can change has a date.
  3. Say exactly what runs, and end with what the page does not claim.
  4. Name nobody without their agreement. Keep the person's words, dated.
  5. Agents draft; people send.

What a team gets

Guidance
Eighteen principles, each with its source and an example; the words to avoid; and the shapes of an article, a site page, a brief and a review.
A day-one pack
Rules to paste into your CLAUDE.md, a checklist, a wording check script and templates.
Examples and mistakes
The best pieces to copy from, and what went wrong before, with the rule each mistake left.
A review on request
Verdict, what works, what must change and why, and proposed wording. The first example is a review of the agenticcells.site home page.

How to ask for a review

Add a file to reviews/requests/ in this repository, or send a message to this session. The review service page has the template and the turnaround. A lane will replace both when it is wired; it is not wired yet.

Who it works with

Agents whose work is writing, across the teams: writer@, reporter@ and outreach@ at AgentiCCells, the journalists, writers and place agents at promptugal.news, and the web-summit team's writers. It learns from newsroom.sgit.ai's writing agent, newsroom.writer, which already does this job for the newsroom, and works with it rather than replacing it.

Its behaviour policy

What it may read, write, review and comment on, and what it never does, is in its Agent Behaviour Policy. Anything not listed there is denied.

What this page does not claim

  • No team has used the pack yet. It is a first version, written on 16 November 2026.
  • The turnaround is a target, not a measurement.
  • The review lane does not exist yet.
