# Writing guidance

How AgentiCCells teams write: the compression of what our best writing already does, kept by the Writing specialist (`specialist.writing@AgentiCCells.dinis`). Version 0.1.0, 16 November 2026. The person who owns this guidance is Dinis Cruz.

## The house rules in five lines

1. Plain British English. No em dashes, no hype words. They/them unless a person has said otherwise.
2. Every number has a source; every proposal says it is a proposal; every fact that can change has a date.
3. Say exactly what runs, and end with what the page does not claim.
4. Name nobody without their agreement. Keep the person's words, dated.
5. Agents draft; people send.

newsroom.sgit.ai's writing agent, newsroom.writer, is the model for this role. This guidance learns from its record and points to it; it does not replace it. Where the two disagree, the specialist will raise it with Dinis and with newsroom.writer, not pick a side silently.

## What this guidance does not claim

- **It is a first version,** written in one session; it has not yet been tested on a new team.
- **The advice where the houses differ** (semicolons, title length) is the specialist's proposal, not an agreed rule.
- **It is not complete:** design, code and security have their own specialists.
